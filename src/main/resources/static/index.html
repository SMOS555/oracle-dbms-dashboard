EER CONSTRUCTS SUMMARY

What EER Added to the ER Model

Weak Entity

ORDER_ITEM

Weak entity identified by owner ORDERS via identifying relationship HAS (double diamond), replacing the M:N CONTAINS. Partial key: ItemNo.

Disjoint Specialization {d}

EMPLOYEE Hierarchy

Total + disjoint specialization splitting EMPLOYEE into MANAGER (Level, Bonus) and DRIVER (LicenseNo, Expiry). Every employee is exactly one of these.

Partial Specialization {d}

PERISHABLE_PRODUCT

Partial specialization of PRODUCT with attributes ShelfLifeDays and StorageTempC. Most products are non-perishable.

Attributes & Recursion

Advanced Attribute Constraints

  • Multivalued Attribute: Phone on SUPPLIER (double ellipse)
  • Composite Attribute: Address on CUSTOMER (Street, City, Pincode)
  • Derived Attribute: TotalAmt on ORDERS (dashed ellipse)
  • Recursive Relationship: SUPERVISES (1 supervisor, N subordinate on EMPLOYEE)
